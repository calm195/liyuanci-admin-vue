## Content review and ordinary restrictions page (2026-10-21)

`/moderation` is lazy-loaded with role menus: review operators and super admins may enter; crisis-only roles are denied. Cases support cursor pages, loading/empty/error/retry, on-demand body and read-only unknown states. Case and content versions are distinct. School-related “unverified” notices bind to the current content version. Trimmed rejection reason/suggestion and explicit confirmation are required. Receipts display content_decision separately from business_outcome. Synthetic content targets remain separate from cases, without inferring real target IDs. Ordinary offline/release actions require a reason, current resource version and confirmation.

`useAdminWorkspace` shares current-role checks, cancellation, session binding, 401/403 cleanup and unmount cleanup; transient page data stays outside stores. Late bodies, receipts and errors cannot overwrite newer selections after switching/paging/leaving. Submissions lock duplicates. Unchanged retries reuse an idempotency key; edited commands receive a new key. A 409 reloads and clears the decision without automatically retrying. Real integration remains disabled.

Component cases cover version notices, approved content with cancelled business execution, rejection requirements, 409, late bodies/receipts, duplicate/failed retries, offline/release, 401/403, revoked permissions, paging/empty results and read-only states.
